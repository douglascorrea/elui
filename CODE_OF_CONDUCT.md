# Code Of Conduct

Elui should be a useful, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Keep criticism focused on code, design, behavior, and project goals.
- Make room for newcomers and different experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption after maintainers ask you to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block
participants whose behavior harms the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to `me@douglascorrea.io`. Maintainers will
review reports as promptly and fairly as possible.
